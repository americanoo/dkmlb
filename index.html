Columns & formulas

Tick the stats to show. Hover a name for what it measures. Counts sit next to their rates (HH beside HardHit%, Chases beside Chase%) so you can check them against PA.

Split columns

Give a stat its own columns for the last 15, 10 and 5 days (and, if you like, vs L / vs R), e.g. HH 15d, HH 10d, HH 5d beside HH. Day windows count back from the most recent game in the loaded data, so pull at least 15 days. Split columns ignore the vs LHP/RHP buttons above the table, sort like any stat, can be rated, and work in formulas as hh__l5, woba__l15, woba__vsR.

Your formula stats

Combine any stats into your own column, e.g. hh + barrels * 2, hh / pa * 100 or z(barrel_pct) + z(hh_per_pa) + itt_eff. It appears in the table, sorts like any stat, and can be added to your rating.

Stats and functions you can use

Click a name to insert it. Operators: + − * / ^ ( ) and comparisons > < >= <= == != (1 if true, 0 if not).

min(a, b, …) max(a, b, …) avg(a, b, …) if(test, a, b) nz(x) round(x, digits) abs sqrt ln clamp(x, lo, hi) z(stat) pctl(stat) rank(stat) scale(stat)

z = standard score vs. everyone on this tab (0 = average), pctl = percentile 0–100, rank = 1 for the highest, scale = 0–1 between lowest and highest. nz(x) turns a missing value into 0. A missing stat makes the result blank unless you wrap it in nz().

Enter the game total (O/U), each team's moneyline, implied team total, and the %-of-bets / %-of-handle on that side. Leave Implied blank to auto-estimate it from the O/U and both moneylines. Data saves automatically and joins to players by DK team abbreviation.

Lookup pulls any player's or team's history from Baseball Savant, so it needs the local stats server running. Double-click start-server.command, then open http://localhost:8000.

Each lookup shows official career and this-season totals from MLB, plus Statcast detail for the last 15, 10 and 5 days — no full-season download.

Filters Change any filter and every result below updates instantly — nothing is re-downloaded.

Rating Weights

You have 10 points to spread across the stats — each point buys up to 1 rating point, so a full allocation rates players 0–10. Maxing one stat at 10 leaves nothing for the rest. ↓ = lower is better.

Data: Baseball Savant statcast (CSV exports or the local pybaseball server) · DraftKings lineup-builder salary CSV. Click a player row to expand their event history. Uploads, Vegas entries, and weights auto-save in this browser (IndexedDB) and reload on your next visit.
